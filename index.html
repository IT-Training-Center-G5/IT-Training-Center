What is IT Training Center?

IT Training Center is a premier technology education and talent accelerator based in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. We offer intensive, industry-crafted courses designed to equip university students, engineers, and career switchers with tangible, highly compensated technical capabilities.

  • Modern and practical curriculum updated quarterly with tech employers
  • Experienced instructors actively working as senior engineers and tech leads
  • Hands-on projects, real client briefs, and live hardware lab practice
  • Dedicated job placement network partnering with 50+ local enterprises
