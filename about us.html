Our Mission

Bridging the Tech Skills Gap in Southeast Asia

Founded in Phnom Penh, IT Training Center (ITTC) was established with a singular vision: to empower ambitious individuals with cutting-edge technical capabilities that meet international industry standards.

Through rigorous project-based learning, mentorship from seasoned software engineers, and strong corporate partnerships, we transform beginners into confident tech professionals ready to innovate.
